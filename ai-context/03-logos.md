# Logos

SVG files are the master source — use PNG for raster contexts only. All assets
live in the `logos/` folder of the brand guidelines site.

## Icon mark

The standalone mark — two waveforms, used wherever the wordmark would be too
much.

| Variant | File | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On gradient | `Thruness-Icon-Light.svg` (white) on hero gradient | App icon, splash screen, social profile |
| On light | `Thruness-Icon-Dark.svg` (#4A2080) | Documents, print, light UI contexts |
| On dark | `Thruness-Icon-Light.svg` (white) | Dark mode, app nav, night ride screen |

## App icon

The full-bleed mark on the brand gradient.

- `Thruness-App-Icon.png` — square master. Submit this to the App Store and
  Play Store; iOS and Android apply their own corner radius.
- `Thruness-App-Icon-Rounded.png` — iOS-style rounded version. Use for
  in-product previews, marketing imagery, and the site favicon.

## Landscape lockup

Icon + wordmark, side by side.

- `Thruness-Landscape-Dark.svg` — On light. Website header, email,
  documents, print.
- `Thruness-Landscape-Light.svg` — On dark. Dark backgrounds, app onboarding,
  dark print stock.

## Portrait lockup

Icon above wordmark, centred.

- `Thruness-Portrait-Dark.svg` — On light. Posters, flyers, show vouchers,
  centred layouts.
- `Thruness-Portrait-Light.svg` — On dark. Hero sections, launch screen,
  dark print.

- `Thruness-Wordmark-Dark.svg` — On light.
- `Thruness-Wordmark-Light.svg` — On dark. App footer, sidebar.

## Mark anatomy

The thicker line beneath is the horse — the foundation that carries the ride.
The lighter line traced over the top is the rider, responsive to the movement
underneath. Together they read as terrain. The peak is the moment of
throughness. Three readings, one mark.

## Clear space and minimum size

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the capital T in the
wordmark. Minimum usage size: 120px wide for the landscape lockup, 40px for
the icon mark alone.

## What not to do

- **Do not stretch or distort.** Always scale proportionally. Never squeeze or
  stretch the mark.
- **Do not recolour the mark.** White, sage deep `#067A60`, or near black
  `#0F1F1A` only.
- **Do not use on clashing backgrounds.** White, warm white, near black, or
  brand gradient only.
- **Do not add effects.** No drop shadows, glows or outlines on the mark
  itself.
- **Do not use wordmark alone at small sizes.** Below 40px use icon mark only.
  The wordmark needs space.
- **Do not place on busy photography.** Ensure sufficient contrast. Use a
  colour overlay if needed.
