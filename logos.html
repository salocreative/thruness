Mark anatomy

The thicker line beneath is the horse — the foundation that carries the ride. The lighter line traced over the top is the rider, responsive to the movement underneath. Together they read as terrain. The peak is the moment of throughness. Three readings, one mark.

Clear space and minimum size

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the capital T in the wordmark. Minimum usage size: 120px wide for the landscape lockup, 40px for the icon mark alone.

What not to do
✕ Do not stretch or distort
Always scale proportionally. Never squeeze or stretch the mark.
✕ Do not recolour the mark
White, sage deep #007525, or near black #0F1F1A only.
✕ Do not use on clashing backgrounds
White, warm white, near black, or brand gradient only.
✕ Do not add effects
No drop shadows, glows or outlines on the mark itself.
✕ Do not use wordmark alone at small sizes
Below 40px use icon mark only. The wordmark needs space.
✕ Do not place on busy photography
Ensure sufficient contrast. Use a colour overlay if needed.
