Brand Story

Who we are, what we believe, and the emotional territory Thruness occupies.

What we are
Thruness is a voice-first gait tracking app for equestrians. It listens while you ride, reads your horse's movement in real time, and builds a picture of the thing that actually matters: the quality of connection between horse and rider.

We exist for the competitive amateur. The rider who has been in the saddle for years, takes their training seriously, and wants to understand what is actually happening underneath them. Not just where they went. How they rode.

But Thruness is about more than data. At its heart it is about the bond between horse and rider. The kind of connection that is felt before it is understood. Riders know it when it happens. That moment when two living things move as one. Thruness exists to help them find it more often, understand it more deeply, and cherish it more completely.

The emotional territory

Calm. Connection. The spiritual bond.

Jen, the founder, describes the relationship between horse and rider as spiritual. That word is deliberate. Thruness is not a fitness tracker with a horse bolted on. It is a tool for people who believe that the bond between horse and rider is something worth paying attention to.

A horse's heart is seven times larger than a human's. That is not just a biological fact. It is a way of understanding what riders already feel. That horses are capable of a depth of connection that goes beyond training. Calmness passes between them. Rhythm is shared. In the best moments, the boundary between the two disappears.

That is throughness. That is what we are named after. And that is what we help riders find.

The mark explained

Two waves. One bond.

The Thruness mark is two waveforms. The thicker line beneath represents the horse — the foundation. The lighter, responsive line traced above represents the rider. When they move together, they form a single unified shape.

The mark has three simultaneous readings:

The bond

Two waveforms finding synchronisation. Horse and rider. One inside the other.

The terrain

A hillside. The landscape they ride through. The natural world the horse belongs to.

The canter

The peak of the mark echoes the asymmetric build-and-float of a canter stride. The gait most associated with true connection.

Who we are not

We are not a yard management app. We are not a social network. We are not a fitness tracker with a horse bolted on. We are not a hardware company. We are not competing with Equilab on their terms.

We occupy different emotional territory. Less training log, more practice partner. Less GPS trace, more felt experience. Less performance metric, more quality of connection.
