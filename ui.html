Colour and weight

Icons inherit colour from their parent text. Stroke width 2 is the default. Use 1.5 at sizes 28px+ for a softer feel. Never recolour an icon to clash with its surface.

Usage
<script src="https://unpkg.com/lucide@latest"></script>

<i data-lucide="play"></i>

<script>lucide.createIcons();</script>
